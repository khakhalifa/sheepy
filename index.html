Sheepy

Simple tools for social-commerce sellers.

About Sheepy

Sheepy is being developed to help small social-commerce sellers manage orders and customer interactions without complicated e-commerce systems.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Sheepy may collect, use, process, and protect information when a customer interacts with a business using Sheepy.

Information We May Process

Depending on the features used by a business, Sheepy may process information contained in customer messages, order information, and technical information required to provide the service.

Instagram and Meta Services

Sheepy may use Meta and Instagram APIs to receive and process messages and other information that a connected business has authorized Sheepy to access.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Sheepy service and to perform actions requested by the connected business.

Information Sharing

Sheepy does not sell customer information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the service.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet legitimate operational requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Data Deletion

A person may request deletion of personal information processed through Sheepy by contacting the business using Sheepy or by contacting Sheepy directly through the contact information made available on this website.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Sheepy develops new features or changes how the service operates.
